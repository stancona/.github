> **From the streets of medieval Iconium to your gaming table.**
> Stancona is a community-centric hobby platform — TTRPG adventures, handcrafted
> artisan miniatures and dice, and bespoke digital design under a single roof.

<br>

## 🎲 What We Do

- **Physical Goods** — high-precision 3D resin miniatures, handcrafted dice, and curated artisan accessories
- **Digital Studio** — original STL kits, TTRPG rulebooks & campaign PDFs, commission design for creators
- **Community** — event playbooks, tabletop culture, and membership crafted around one feeling: *"these folks did this right"*
